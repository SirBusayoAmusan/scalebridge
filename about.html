About Scalebridge

We believe economic progress should translate into better opportunities and better livelihoods.

When critical infrastructure is inadequate, businesses struggle to access capital and productive opportunities remain unrealised. Scalebridge exists to help close these gaps by directing capital towards projects, businesses and infrastructure that can strengthen economies and improve lives.

Vision

To contribute to economies where critical infrastructure enables productivity, businesses have the capital to grow, opportunities are created, and people can build better and more secure livelihoods.

Mission

To originate, structure, finance and mobilise capital for infrastructure and economically productive opportunities that create sustainable financial value, strengthen economic capacity and improve livelihoods.
